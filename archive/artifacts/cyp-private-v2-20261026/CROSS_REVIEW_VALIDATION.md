# Private peer review from the validation/lineage audit

Reviewed `HYPOTHESES_DATA.md` and `HYPOTHESES_SOLUTIONS.md`. No experiment, fit, acquisition, rental or public action was launched by this review.

**First launch recommendation: V1, combined with the nonparametric part of OS-H3.** The matched cached banks are already available, and this can distinguish distribution/operator effects cheaply. Use identity, one predeclared moment map and one predeclared empirical-quantile map; preserve exact hashes/IDs and all five metrics. Primary replay uses the actual served lane and its matching saved incumbent counterpart; raw-anchor replay is a separate diagnostic. The blinded vectors remain unscored. Matching an incumbent distribution or minimizing drift is not an accuracy objective. A local gain on two reused cohorts does not independently qualify a replacement.

**OS-H1: conditional launch after a bounded implementation smoke.** This is a meaningful interaction test and does not merely repeat our old coupled loss package. The four arms must hold the checkpoint *metric implementation* constant: using the name `val_loss` in all arms is insufficient if its underlying objective changes. Fix architecture/initialization, target/bound transforms, auxiliary population and per-target task weights. Audit actual inverse-variance row weights because the current source replaces them with ones. Use genuine L1 interval distance; keep auxiliary targets without valid bounds on an explicitly unchanged loss. Pair seed/device logs and retain all outcomes—the unchanged union-fold rerun demonstrates that nominal seed alone does not define an artifact. A one-fold smoke proves implementation, not the scientific comparison. The author's effect size is useful motivation, not our expected gain.

**D1: bounded acquisition plus a CPU pilot is justified, with a stronger matched-record control.** Match compound membership, activity type and per-compound effective record weight. Otherwise retaining multiple context-specific observations while comparing with one pooled median changes measurement multiplicity as well as conditioning. Include a simple source/probe-intercept comparator and an explicit unknown-context state before high-cardinality assay interactions. Keep true inequalities as bounds, not exact concentrations or invented confidence intervals. A source/probe improvement may be valuable without a kinetic/mechanistic interpretation.

**D2 and OS-H2 should be one shared trial, not duplicate experiments.** The free auxiliary head is the necessary practical control; compare it with the honest empirical same-potency link and the condition-separated latent. Match capacity and total contribution of correlated direct/TDI/derived-shift losses. A signed difference derived from two observed potencies is not an independent new measurement, and its uncertainty cannot automatically be formed by assuming independent posteriors. Keep Emax and single-concentration scales as documented, and cross-fit any auxiliary predictions used as inputs. The CI/plate-quality control is needed to distinguish metabolic-fate signal from estimation noise. A negative sign is permitted; enrichment among weak curves alone does not establish deactivation.

**D3: useful orthogonal data hypothesis, but stage it after overlap feasibility.** A fixed, outcome-blind sample of overlapping compounds can test whether the counter-assay supplies usable information. Preserve inactive/inconclusive/missing distinctions and concentration/technology provenance. Filtering should not indiscriminately delete a compound's trustworthy fluorescence/MS labels because a luminescent source may be confounded. Keep dual-activity cases. A wrong-technology control tests the proposed technology-specific factorization; equal effects do not conclusively rule out broader promiscuity or multi-technology interference. This branch should not become an unbounded364k-compound download before overlap is established.

**OS-H3's learned signed correction is a later branch, not part of the first V1 replay.** It is plausible only when there is additional conditional ordering information and a legitimate common bank. Large cancelling coefficients are a real competing failure mode. The current H7 result did not justify an extra global expert, and author caches may have trained on our outer query molecules. Do not call such caches a matched outer benchmark. Keep regularization and untouched-column constraints explicit, and do not assign the author's latest live score to a different released revision/cache.

## Common validation correction

“Keep the exact incumbent fixed” has two roles that must not be conflated. It is the correct deployment/retention control. It cannot be used as an unseen predictor on new validation molecules that were in its full training set. Existing legitimate outer counterparts can support conditional comparisons on their existing cohorts; new dense-series or external-assay validation needs eligible out-of-sample banks, with that cost stated. Public teacher pretraining membership remains a separate unknown.
