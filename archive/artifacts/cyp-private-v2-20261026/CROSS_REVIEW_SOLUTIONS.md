# Source-audit peer review of the first wave

Private review of `HYPOTHESES_DATA.md`, `HYPOTHESES_VALIDATION.md` and `HYPOTHESES_SOLUTIONS.md`, 26 October 2026. No experiment launched by this reviewer.

- **V1 — launch the fixed CPU replay.** Lock both candidates and all three operators before scoring, preserve the exact349165 incumbent, and report raw-input and served-input lanes separately. A tie-aware midrank map does not necessarily reproduce the incumbent's entire empirical distribution when candidate ties differ; report tie counts and verify both self-map controls. Neither proximity to the incumbent nor restored moments is an accuracy result. Keep blind vectors unscored.
- **V3 — launch structural feasibility only.** Use disjoint seed and query-candidate pools; no query numeric outcome may rank/select seeds. Match reference-set size and assess support against each CYP's actually labeled training set, not only all4,905 structures. Report label availability, source strata, effective series counts and achievable density; do not force750 rows.
- **V3 model fitting — hold until feasibility passes.** Fresh validation counterparts must exclude query compounds and audited equivalents from every trainable primary/auxiliary source. Full incumbent checkpoints already saw released training labels and cannot supply honest predictions for a newly carved holdout. Keep the matched-count removal control so losing training volume is not mistaken for losing relevant neighbors.
- **D1 — launch the bounded data pilot.** Freeze compound membership first. Compare pooled **exact-IC50** versus context-aware exact-IC50 before adding censoring as a separate factor. Keep AC50/Ki separate, preserve unknown context, group repeated compound measurements across folds, and report attrition by organism/probe/preparation/qualifier. Otherwise an apparent context gain can be a compound-selection or endpoint-curation gain.
- **OS-H1 — launch numerical audits and a bounded runtime smoke before the full factorial.** Freeze architecture/init, data, auxiliary scaling, precision and checkpoint criterion across the four cells. Verify exact L1 interval distance in train and evaluation units, per-target absent-bound handling, and that dummy bound columns do not dilute loss denominators. Public scripts contain stale comments and one squared-hinge implementation; the prior confounded private loss run is not a clean negative result for this interaction.
- **D2 / OS-H2 — begin with the condition-aware data diagnosis, not a rigid shared-potency Hill link.** The public same-π model couples a +NADPH screen to the −NADPH direct target, uses globally fitted nuisance constants, and does not consume signed Emax as a fraction. Its own code reports rank improvement with potency-scale damage. A free auxiliary-head control and separate condition-specific latents are required before interpreting any effect mechanistically; do not call fitted effective `E` the released Emax.
- **D3 — useful later discrimination, not mandatory before wave one.** Keep it bounded to verified overlapping reporter/CYP records and retain dual-activity cases. A reporter flag alone cannot distinguish interference from true promiscuous inhibition. The wrong-technology loading is more informative than a generic permutation control.
- **Interpretation boundary.** No new leaderboard probes, public outputs or automatic promotion. The two old cohorts remain reused diagnostics; matched private panels and fixed serving operators address different uncertainties. Preserve adverse outcomes and effect attribution, rather than adding a universal gain/significance threshold.
