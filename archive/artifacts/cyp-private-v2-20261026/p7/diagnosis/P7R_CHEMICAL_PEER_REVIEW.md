# Chemical peer review of proposed P7R

The four-arm contrast—zero descriptors, original standardized120, mask onlyVol/Dense, bounded training-CDF120—is defensible as a **diagnostic of narrow descriptor defects versus broader feature conditioning**. It is not a clean proof that CDF “repairs” chemical meaning. Preserve the same rows and labels: this is the original **P7 eighteen-task recipe**, not the new P8 condition experiment. Keep P7 targets, architecture, initial weights, folds, optimizer, loss coefficients and stopping unchanged across these arms.

1. **Vol/Dense-only masking has a strong factual basis.** The mixed-unit radius equation reproduces all85extreme outputs and200ordinary controls. Masking both columns in standardized space removes the implicated direct value and its dependent ratio without declaring the85molecules invalid. It also removes valid volume/density information from ordinary molecules. A gain therefore supports the net harm of these coordinates in this recipe, not the proposition that volume is chemically irrelevant.
2. **There is evidence for a broader scaling hypothesis.** Across training folds,25of120columns reach|z|>10 and54reach|z|>5. Besides Vol53.1 and Dense16.9, maxima include VDss132.7, Flex39.0, MDCK38.0, formal charge28.0, nRot23.4, nStereo21.4, boiling point20.9 andTPSA20.1. These thresholds are descriptive diagnostics, not validity/success cutoffs. The source cases differ: some are unconstrained model extrapolations, some rare but meaningful chemical size/charge/topology. Do not label every largez a data error.
3. **Keep the CDF bounded after transformation.** A concrete training empirical midrank CDF, mapped to[-1,1], preserves ties and order while limiting coordinate leverage. Use deterministic tie handling, no jitter, and an explicitly documented outside-range convention. Do not StandardScale the CDF outputs again: that can re-amplify rare discrete values and defeat the intended bound. Fit only the exact post-purge training rows; persist support/tie counts and transform. Constant-column behavior must be defined even though no current training column is constant.
4. **CDF changes more than outliers.** It replaces physical distances and probability spacing with empirical ranks, may expand compressed CYP probability tails, and changes relative input amplitude. It cannot recover distinctions already tied at1 or correct the wrong chemical ranking of unit-corrupted Vol values. Its empirical distribution reflects the mixed official/external training pool, not an assay-specific physical calibration. An improvement would support this conditioning choice; it would not identify a single biochemical mechanism.
5. **Interpret the contrasts separately.** Original→Vol/Dense-masked tests the narrow numeric defect. Original→CDF tests a bundled robustness/rank-representation intervention across120columns. Masked versusCDF distinguishes the narrow removal from broader remapping but is not a strict factorial: CDF also remaps Vol/Dense rather than removes them. Report that limitation instead of adding new arms now. Zero versus original remains the practical matched reference.
6. **Use evidence beyond “there are outliers.”** Track per-arm optimization trajectories, direct/auxiliary behavior and allfive official metrics/perCYP. The trained frozen-mask intervention is only a dependence diagnostic; it cannot undo historical encoder updates. A retrained improvement with boundedCDF could arise from optimization or changed function geometry even if no single feature was biologically wrong.
7. **Retain the chemical/assay caveats.** CYP probabilities carry global signal but do not measure quantitative direct pIC50; substrate and inhibitor are different tasks. Other predicted ADMET endpoints may supply useful correlated chemistry or harmful assay-mismatched priors. The available120pair analysis does not support blanket loss of large potency-cliff ordering. No hand-repair, source remapping, new teacher requests or row removal is needed for the proposed contrast.
