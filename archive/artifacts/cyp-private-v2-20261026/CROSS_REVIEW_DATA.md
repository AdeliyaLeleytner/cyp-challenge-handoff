### V1 and V2

V1 is a useful operator diagnostic, not a biological correction. The positive-affine moment map preserves ranking except ties, so it cannot explain broad rank degradation. Quantile anchoring can alter ties/local spacing while inheriting the anchor’s mistakes. The plan correctly keeps the served lane primary and acknowledges poor local placement. Require absolute baseline/candidate metrics and activity/interval strata alongside paired differences; do not promote a map solely because its distribution resembles349165.

V2 is essential before blaming biological features for the public regression. Keeping a coefficient fixed isolates a conditional artifact contrast; refitting that coefficient is a separate factor. The old/new outer banks have different purge histories, so neither their difference nor the32 inner exclusions is a pure chemical-curation effect. Preserve the unchanged-fold evidence and do not label a base replacement as “feature addition.” No new A/A neural fit is presently needed unless a concrete unresolved variation question changes the choice.

### V3

The feasibility-first design is appropriate. The530 no-released-single/direct-only rows have only3A4 direct labels; they are a coverage stratum, not proof of a particular chemical library, concentration or non-hit status. Missingness may reflect several selection/QC/release mechanisms. Balance/report observed per-CYP cells, low/informative intervals and this stratum without using query outcome values to select seeds. Keep parent-retained, series-withheld and matched-count random-removal comparisons on identical query IDs/masks. Withhold every experimental auxiliary endpoint and audited chemical equivalent of a query from fitting; a full incumbent trained on those compounds cannot validate a fresh panel.

A feasible panel may be smaller or less dense than750. That is useful evidence about what the public data can support, not permission to fill missing cells or call proxy clusters the organizer’s75 series. Do not make prototype ranking depend on a candidate pool’s hidden numerical outcomes. The different3A4/2D6 probe/readout contexts also argue for per-endpoint interpretations rather than one undifferentiated “test-like” geometry score.

### OS-H1

The loss×weighting interaction is a real question and the matched factorial is stronger than another objective-only swap. Reported posterior/curve-fit std is not automatically independent experimental replicate variance. Interval tolerance and inverse-variance weighting can privilege the same narrow-band population twice. Also, an L1 interval loss is not automatically the entire official ST-RAE unless its soft-threshold and endpoint normalization are reproduced; name the actual surrogate precisely.

Before paid fitting, check per-target missing-label/bound masks, standardized-bound transforms, reduction/head normalization and gradients. Keep the same checkpoint selection criterion across all arms and retain the exact deployment incumbent. A one-fold runtime/numerical test does not establish the20-job interaction. Inspect allfive metrics by potency/interval/support strata; a lower average from de-emphasizing uncertain weak compounds alone is not a broad biological improvement.

### OS-H2 and D2

The condition-correct/free-head contrast is promising, but the source’s same-potency screen equation cannot presently be called a mechanistic observation likelihood. The single screen is +NADPH; the target is −NADPH preincubation. The actual recorded single concentration is49.505µM, while nominal/preincubation dilution remains unresolved. Released Emax is signed near−1, unlike a fitted positive suppression parameter. Do not infer direct IC50 bounds or substitute Emax into a0–1 fraction.

A free auxiliary head is ready as a statistical comparator. Any empirical E/H calibration must be fitted only in training folds. A condition-specific latent can be tested using paired direct/active measurements, with signed shifts and no forced TDI≥direct inequality. Retain the alternative explanation that auxiliary gains reflect shared curve-fit/plate QC rather than metabolic mechanism. No acquisition of additional ADMET predictions is needed.

### OS-H3

Incumbent-anchored corrections can be tested cheaply once a genuinely matched bank exists. The source’s cache is not automatically a heldout predictor on our cohorts. Keep untouched columns byte-identical, separate correction fitting from selection/evaluation, and compare signed corrections to convex alternatives. Full-query moment/quantile preservation says nothing guaranteed about the unknown scored series subset. This route tests ordering/correction portability; it does not resolve assay-context mismatch or a weak validation regime by itself.

## D1 acquisition contract ready for root authorization

Start with at most1,000–3,000 records from a few explicitly selected assay contexts, not all databases. Existing verified paths/counts support DBOMF3A4, dextromethorphan2D6 and contrasting drug-probe/HLM contexts. Retrieve assay metadata before deciding whether to treat a value as experimental human inhibition. Target-ID filtering alone demonstrably admits guinea-pig and in-silico examples. Preserve all exclusions and their reasons rather than quietly calling them negatives.

Use authored IC50 inequalities only after type/unit/context checks. Do not obtain direct bounds from single log2fc, Emax, blank cells or merged TDI-negative labels. Keep AC50/Potency/Ki as separate endpoints. The CHEMBL10/1A2 mixed-AC50/IC50 reconstruction is a positive curation control. Match compound membership first when testing pooled versus context-preserved targets; add censored observations as a second factor. This separates biological estimand information from extra chemical coverage.

Required row contract: source/version/record ID; original and prepared structures; actual assay and target species; enzyme preparation; probe and known concentrations; inhibitor value/unit/relation; NADPH/preincubation/reaction context; response/fit/direction/QC; document and duplicate lineage. Unknowns remain unknown. No new unique-compound or independent-measurement total is asserted before de-duplication.

All first-wave work remains private. These readiness judgments authorize no public disclosure or leaderboard dispatch and introduce no arbitrary score/significance thresholds.
